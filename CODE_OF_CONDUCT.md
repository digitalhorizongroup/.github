# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Digital Horizon Group is committed to a professional and respectful technical community.

Expected behavior includes:

- communicating clearly and respectfully;
- giving constructive, specific feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and viewpoints;
- keeping technical discussions relevant;
- accepting correction and taking responsibility for mistakes.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks;
- discriminatory or degrading comments;
- spam, unsolicited promotion, or repeated off-topic messages;
- publishing another person's private information;
- sharing malicious code, credentials, or private data;
- disrupting discussions or moderation.

## Scope

This policy applies in organization repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and official Digital Horizon Group community spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct problems privately to [hello@digitalhorizon.group](mailto:hello@digitalhorizon.group). Include relevant links, screenshots, dates, and context when possible.

Do not start a public argument or disclose private information while reporting an incident.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or permanently remove a participant. Decisions will consider context, impact, repeated behavior, and community safety.

Reports will be reviewed as privately as practical. Retaliation against a person who reports a problem in good faith is not permitted.
